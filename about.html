About Rehman Auto Industry

Every bike starts with our name on the line.

We're a motorbike assembly workshop on Hasilpur Road, Bahawalpur — built by people who'd rather do a job right than do it twice. Here's who we are, how we work, and why hundreds of riders and dealers trust what leaves our floor.

Hand Assembly

Every unit is built frame-up by trained technicians, torqued to spec at each stage.

Quality Checked

A two-point inspection, mid-build and final, before anything leaves the workshop.

Genuine Parts

Sourced only through vetted suppliers — no shortcuts, no grey-market swaps.

Our story

Started small, built to last.

Rehman Auto Industry began as a small assembly bay on Hasilpur Road, Bahawalpur, with a handful of technicians and one rule that never changed: nothing leaves the floor unless it's built the right way. What started as local orders from nearby riders slowly grew into steady dealer relationships across the district.

Today we assemble hundreds of units a year, from single custom builds to full dealer fleet orders, but the workshop still runs on the same principle it opened with — every bike gets built like it's going out under our own name, because it is.

  • Founded on Hasilpur Road, Bahawalpur
  • Grown through repeat dealer trust, not shortcuts
  • Still hand-assembling every single unit
Our workshop

A floor built around the build, not around us.

Our workshop is laid out as a fixed sequence of stations — frame, engine, electricals, body, wheels, and final inspection — so every bike moves through the same checkpoints in the same order, whether it's one unit or twenty. Each station is stocked with the tools and torque references specific to that stage, which keeps the work consistent no matter which technician is on it that day.

We keep a dedicated bay for fleet and dealer orders separate from custom single builds, so batch work never slows down someone waiting on their one bike, and vice versa.

01

From intake to inspection.

Every order starts with a build sheet listing the exact model, spec, and parts required. From there the unit moves station by station — frame and engine first, then electricals and body panels, then wheels, brakes, and suspension — with a technician signing off at each stage before the bike advances.

Nothing skips a station, and nothing gets rushed to hit a delivery date. If a stage isn't ready to be signed off, the bike waits until it is.

  • Build sheet logged for every unit
  • Fixed station order, no skipped steps
  • Sign-off required before advancing
02

Checked twice, not once.

Midway through assembly, a technician other than the one building the unit runs a torque and alignment check. At final handover, a second, more thorough inspection covers brakes, electricals, fluid levels, and overall fit and finish before the bike is cleared for delivery.

Every inspection is recorded against that unit's build sheet, so if a question ever comes up down the line, we can trace exactly what was checked, by whom, and when.

Our people

Technicians who know the bike, not just the checklist.

Every technician on our floor trains on the specific fitment and torque spec of the models we assemble before they're signed off to work a station unsupervised. Most of our senior team has been with us for years, which means the person tightening your headset bearing has probably done it a thousand times before.

We keep the team small enough that everyone knows the standard by heart, and big enough to take on fleet orders without cutting corners on turnaround.
