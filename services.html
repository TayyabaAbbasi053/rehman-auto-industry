Built For The Road Ahead

What we offer

Three services, one standard of work.

From the first bolt to the final handover, every bike is hand-assembled, inspected twice, and built with genuine parts — no shortcuts, no exceptions.

01

Bike Assembly

Every unit that leaves our floor is built by hand, frame up, by technicians trained on the exact fitment and torque spec for each model — no shortcuts, no rushed stations. Each bike moves through a fixed sequence of build stations, and no unit advances to the next stage until the current one is fully signed off, so the process stays consistent whether it's a single custom order or a fleet batch of twenty.

  • Frame, engine & body fitted by hand
  • Torqued to manufacturer spec
  • Logged at every build stage
02

Quality Inspection

Nothing leaves the floor until it's checked twice — once mid-build, once at final handover — covering torque, alignment, brakes, and electricals. Every check is recorded against the unit's build sheet, so if a question ever comes up after delivery, we can trace exactly what was inspected, by whom, and when.

  • Mid-build & final inspection
  • Brake, torque & alignment checks
  • Signed off before it ships
03

Spare Parts Sourcing

We source genuine and OEM-equivalent parts through vetted suppliers only, so every bike — and every repair after it — runs on parts you can trust. Whether it's a full engine rebuild or a single replacement bolt, our sourcing team checks each part against the manufacturer's spec before it ever reaches the workshop floor.

  • Genuine & OEM-equivalent parts
  • Vetted supplier network
  • No grey-market swaps

Quality bike assembly since day one.
